As a core member, I built the edge-side inference framework for an intelligent in-vehicle scenario, forming a closed loop from visual perception to decision making.

- **YOLO11** detects the driver's face and key facial regions
- **ViT** models fatigue-related visual features and outputs a fatigue-state assessment
- Fatigue assessment is linked with **risk warning and intelligent trip planning**

**Deployment**
- Huawei Atlas 200I DK with NPU inference acceleration (CANN / MindSpore Lite)
- Real-time fatigue-state evaluation at **15+ FPS**

**Award:** 2nd Prize (Provincial), 2025 Huawei Ascend AI Innovation Competition (Liaoning).
