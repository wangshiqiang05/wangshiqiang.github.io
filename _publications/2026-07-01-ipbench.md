Intellectual Property (IP) is a highly specialized domain that integrates technical and legal knowledge, making it inherently complex and knowledge-intensive. To bridge the gap of existing datasets and benchmarks that focus narrowly on patents or cover limited aspects of the IP field, we introduce **IPBench**, the first comprehensive IP task taxonomy and a large-scale bilingual benchmark encompassing **8 IP mechanisms and 20 distinct tasks**, designed to evaluate LLMs in real-world IP practice.

We benchmark 19 mainstream LLMs, ranging from general-purpose to domain-specific and from chat-oriented to reasoning-focused models, under zero-shot, few-shot, and chain-of-thought settings. Our results show that even the top-performing model achieves only 75.8% accuracy, indicating significant room for improvement. We publicly release the data and code, and will expand IPBench with additional tasks to better reflect real-world complexities.
